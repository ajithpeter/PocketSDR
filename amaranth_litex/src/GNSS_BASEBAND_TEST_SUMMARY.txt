To synthesize the module for ECP5-45F:

1. Generate Verilog from RTLIL (requires Yosys):
   $ yosys -p 'read_rtlil /tmp/gnss_baseband_test.rtlil; synth_ecp5 -json design.json'

2. Place & Route (requires nextpnr):
   $ nextpnr-ecp5 --json design.json --config ecp5-45f.lpf --textcfg bitstream.config

3. Generate Bitstream (requires ecppack):
   $ ecppack bitstream.config bitstream.bit

4. Program FPGA:
   $ openFPGALoader --device=ecp5 bitstream.bit
